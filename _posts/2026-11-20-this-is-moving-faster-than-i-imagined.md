Last week, a recruiter called me about an open position. I figured, why not give it a try? I need to practice interviewing anyway.

After that first call, I met with the department head on Wednesday. My next conversation was this morning, and it went really well. Honestly, I've had good conversations with everyone I've talked to at this company so far.

Right after that call, the recruiter reached out again to let me know they're moving me forward to the next round: a panel interview. They also sent me a project to present. Presenting isn't my strong suit, so I'm nervous about that part, but I'm going to give it my best shot.

What's really getting me is how fast this is all moving, much faster than I ever imagined. I think I understand why. It's the end of the year, and from what I've seen at my last company, when there's budget left to hire, they'd rather use it than lose it.

So, we'll see how it goes. I'm nervous and anxious because this goal of being employed is finally getting closer, and I'm really hoping it all works out.

Hopefully my next post is good news.
